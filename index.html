HOW IT WORKS

A small, repeatable ecosystem.

PlantForge grows a seeded branch skeleton on each pass, then sends leaf transforms to WebGL instancing. Falling leaves never travel through JavaScript: a deterministic phase, wind vector, and gravity are evaluated per particle in the GPU vertex shader.
